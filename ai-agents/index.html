What is AI agent development?

AI agent development is building software that reads a request, works out what needs to happen and then does it inside your systems. A chatbot answers questions. An agent answers, then checks the calendar, books the slot, creates the lead in your CRM, sends the confirmation and tells a person when something falls outside its rules. That second half, the doing, is where the value sits and where most of the engineering effort goes.

This is what people mean by agentic AI: a language model that is allowed to call tools such as "check availability", "create contact" or "draft invoice", with firm limits on what it may do on its own. For the longer comparison, read our guide to the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot.

What can an AI agent do for a business?

The useful test is simple: what has changed in your systems by the end of the conversation? If the answer is "nothing, a person still has to retype it", you have a chatbot. Typical actions in a Crank Labs agent:

  • Answer enquiries in seconds, at any hour, from information your team has approved.
  • Qualify the lead by asking the questions your best salesperson asks: budget band, timeline, location, product and who decides.
  • Book the next step into a shared calendar, with confirmations and reminders.
  • Update the CRM with the contact, source, answers and a short summary, so nobody retypes a conversation.
  • Route and escalate to the right person or branch, with the full context attached.
  • Follow up on quotes that went quiet and on past customers who are due for a service, within each channel's opt-in rules.

We build on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, never on unofficial tools that put your number at risk. A conversation the customer starts can be answered freely within the service window; messages you start, such as reminders and reactivation, need approved templates and an opt-in. We design around those rules from the first day. For a sector example, see how UAE clinics use WhatsApp AI agents.

AI voice agents

Voice is less forgiving than text. Callers interrupt, accents vary and a two-second pause feels long. A good AI voice agent keeps its turns short, reads key details back to the caller and hands over to a person the moment the call turns complex or emotional. We recommend starting voice on well-defined call types: bookings, rescheduling, opening hours and order status.

Email and internal operations agents

These work out of sight and often pay back fastest. An agent that sorts a shared mailbox and drafts replies for a person to send, or one that answers "where is order X" from your own systems, removes dozens of small interruptions a day. When the work is mostly moving data between systems rather than conversation, it is better framed as AI automation of invoices, approvals and reporting.

How does an AI agent build work?

  1. Diagnose. A free working session where we map how enquiries arrive, who handles them, where they wait and which systems hold the truth.
  2. Design the conversation and the decisions. We write down what the agent may answer, which questions it must ask, what it may do on its own and what always goes to a person.
  3. Connect the systems. Calendar, CRM, inbox, accounting or a custom database, each with access limited to what the workflow needs.
  4. Test on real history. We replay past conversations and awkward cases against the agent before any customer sees it: voice notes, typos, mixed languages, angry messages.
  5. Launch with supervision. The agent goes live with your team reading transcripts and correcting it, usually on one channel, one branch or out-of-hours traffic first.
  6. Run and tune. We measure response time, handover rate, bookings and errors, then adjust rules, content and integrations. The next channel comes once the first one pays for itself.

A focused agent, such as WhatsApp lead response for one business line, is typically live in two to four weeks. Agents that touch several systems are phased so results show from the first month.

What guardrails should an AI agent have?

An agent that can take actions needs firmer limits than one that only talks. These are the guardrails we treat as standard, and the ones you should ask any supplier about:

  • Approved sources only. Prices, policies and product facts come from content your team signed off, not from the model's general knowledge.
  • Scoped actions. Each tool has a narrow job. An agent may create a booking but not cancel a day's diary. It may draft a refund note but not issue the refund.
  • Human approval for high-stakes steps. In finance and healthcare, a person approves before anything is sent or posted.
  • Least access. Agents run on infrastructure Crank Labs controls or on your own cloud, and see only the data each workflow needs.
  • Full logs. Every message, decision and action is recorded, so you can audit what happened and why.
  • Privacy by design. Consent, retention and access are planned with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 in mind.

How human handover should work

Handover is a feature, not a failure. Set clear triggers: the customer asks for a person, the topic is clinical, legal or a complaint, the agent is unsure, or the deal is above a value you choose. When a trigger fires, the right person gets a notification with a summary and the full transcript, the customer is told who will reply and roughly when, and the agent stops talking. The worst design is a handover into an inbox nobody watches.

Why do AI agents fail in production?

Agents that disappoint rarely fail because the model is weak. They fail for ordinary operational reasons:

  • No system access. The agent can chat but cannot see the calendar or write to the CRM, so staff still do the real work.
  • Messy source information. Out-of-date price lists and contradictory policies produce confident wrong answers.
  • No owner. Nobody on the business side reads transcripts or approves changes, so small problems pile up unseen.
  • Demo-only testing. It handled ten friendly questions and breaks on voice notes, half-finished messages and customers who change their mind.
  • Channel rules ignored. Outbound WhatsApp messages sent without templates or opt-in get blocked, and the number's quality rating suffers.
  • The wrong measure. Counting conversations instead of bookings, qualified leads or hours saved hides whether it is working.

What should you ask an AI agent development company?

  • Which of our systems will the agent read from and write to, and with what permissions?
  • What exactly happens when the agent does not know the answer?
  • Can we see the transcripts and the action log, and who reviews them each week?
  • How will you test it on our real conversations before launch?
  • Who fixes it when a price list, supplier or API changes?
  • Where does our customer data live, and can it run on our own cloud?

Red flags: a polished demo with no integration behind it, a promise that the agent never needs a human, unofficial WhatsApp tools, no plan for monitoring after launch, and a fixed quote given before anyone has looked at your process.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build an AI agent?

A focused agent that handles one workflow, such as WhatsApp lead response or appointment booking for one business line, is typically live in two to four weeks. Agents that read from and write to several systems are phased, so the first part goes live early and results show from the first month while the rest is built.

Will an AI agent replace my sales or front desk team?

It should not, and we do not design it to. The agent takes the repetitive first reply, the qualifying questions and the booking, which frees your people for the conversations that close deals or need judgement. Every agent hands over to a named person when a customer asks for one or when the case falls outside its rules.

Can an AI agent connect to our existing CRM and calendar?

Usually, yes. Crank Labs builds around the tools you already use rather than replacing them, including popular CRMs, Microsoft 365, Gmail, calendars, Zoho Books, TallyPrime and custom databases. If a system has no usable API, we look at alternatives such as a shared database, email-based updates or a small connector built for that system.

Do we need the WhatsApp Business Platform for a WhatsApp AI agent?

For a production agent, yes. The official WhatsApp Business Platform is the supported route for automated messaging, and it sets the rules on approved templates and customer opt-in for messages your business starts. Unofficial tools that automate a normal WhatsApp account risk the number being restricted, so we do not use them.

Is our customer data safe with an AI agent?

Agents run on infrastructure Crank Labs controls or on your own cloud, with access limited to what each workflow needs. Every action is logged. For finance and healthcare workflows, a person approves before anything is sent or posted. Consent and retention are planned with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 in mind.

What is the difference between agentic AI and workflow automation?

Workflow automation follows fixed steps you define in advance, which suits invoices, approvals and reports. Agentic AI handles open-ended input, such as a customer message, decides which step comes next and then calls the right tools. Many good builds combine both: an agent handles the conversation and a fixed workflow handles the posting and reporting behind it.

Can an AI agent handle Arabic, Hindi and English?

Current language models handle these languages and the mixed messages customers often send. The harder part is approved content: prices, policies and replies in each language should be reviewed by someone on your team who speaks it. We test the agent on real conversations in the languages your customers actually use before it goes live.
