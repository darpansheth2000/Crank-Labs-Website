AEO vs SEO: How to Get Recommended by ChatGPT and AI Search

AEO vs SEO explained for business owners: how ChatGPT and AI Overviews choose who to recommend, what to change on your site and how to track AI visibility.

The short answer

SEO helps your pages rank in search results and earn a click. AEO (answer engine optimisation) helps your business become the answer that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews give, and the name they recommend. You win at AEO with pages that lead with clear answers, question-style headings, structured data, consistent business facts across the web and independent sources that mention you. Good technical SEO is still the foundation.

The short version of AEO vs SEO: SEO is about ranking a page so a person clicks it, while AEO, answer engine optimisation, is about being the business an AI assistant names when someone asks it for a recommendation. The two share a foundation, but they reward different things. SEO rewards relevance and authority at page level. AEO rewards clear, quotable answers and facts about your business that several independent sources agree on. If your buyers have started asking ChatGPT or Perplexity "who should I hire for this?", you need both.

Why does AEO matter now?

Buyers increasingly ask an assistant before they search. "Which dental clinic in JLT is good for implants?" "Who builds WhatsApp bots for businesses in Dubai?" "Which companies manufacture fire-fighting vehicles in India?" The assistant replies with a short list and a few sentences about each name. If you are not on that list, there is no page two to fall back on and often no click at all, because the buyer got what they needed from the answer.

That changes what marketing has to achieve. A page that ranks fifth on Google still gets some traffic. A business that is missing from the AI answer gets nothing from that conversation, however good its website is.

What is the difference between AEO and SEO?

They are not rivals. AEO is built on top of SEO, but the goal, the unit you optimise and the way you measure success are different.

DimensionSEOAEO
GoalA high ranking and a clickA citation and a recommendation by name
Unit you optimiseThe pageThe answer: a paragraph, a table row, a fact that can be quoted
Where you appearBlue links, maps, featured snippetsChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Overviews, voice assistants
Main signalsRelevance, links, technical health, user behaviourClarity, structure, consistent facts, independent mentions, freshness
What success looks likeTraffic and enquiries from searchBeing named, described accurately and linked when buyers ask
How you measureRankings, clicks, conversionsHow often you are mentioned, your position, which sources are cited

Some people call the AI part GEO, generative engine optimisation. The labels overlap almost completely. What matters is the work, not the acronym.

How do ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews decide who to recommend?

None of these engines publishes its ranking rules, so anyone who claims to know the exact formula is guessing. What you can observe, by running the same questions week after week, is a consistent pattern with three stages.

1. Retrieval: can the engine find you?

Many assistants search the live web through their own crawlers or a search index before they answer. If your pages are blocked, slow, rendered only by JavaScript or buried in PDFs, they are not in the pool the assistant reads from. This is where SEO and AEO are the same job.

2. Selection: can the engine quote you?

From what it retrieves, the model picks passages that answer the question cleanly. A paragraph that says "We are a dental clinic in JLT offering implants, aligners and whitening, open until 10pm" is easy to use. A paragraph that says "We deliver excellence in smiles" is not.

3. Corroboration: do other sources agree?

Assistants are more willing to recommend a business when several independent sources describe it the same way: its own site, its Google Business Profile, directories, reviews, press and industry listings. Contradictions, such as two addresses or different service lists, make you a riskier name to suggest.

Does SEO still matter if buyers ask ChatGPT?

Yes. Crawlability, page speed, clean URLs, internal links, sitemaps and a sensible site structure are what let any engine find and understand your content. Google AI Overviews draws on Google's own index, so classic SEO directly feeds it. Treat SEO as the plumbing and AEO as what you put through it. Skipping the plumbing to chase AI answers is the most common way AEO projects stall.

How do you get your business recommended by ChatGPT?

This is the eight-step framework we use when we run answer engine optimisation for a business. Work through it in order, because the early steps make the later ones count.

  1. Check crawler access. Make sure your robots.txt and firewall do not block the crawlers used by the assistants your buyers use, such as GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot for OpenAI and PerplexityBot for Perplexity, as well as Googlebot. Many sites block them by accident through a security plugin or a hosting default.
  2. Lead every key page with the answer. Put a two or three sentence direct answer at the top of each service page and post, in plain language that still makes sense if it is quoted on its own.
  3. Write headings as questions. Use the exact questions buyers ask: "How much does a dental implant cost in Dubai?", "How long does a WhatsApp agent take to set up?". Then answer each one in the first sentence under it.
  4. Publish a clear facts page. One page that states who you are, what you do, where you operate, which sectors you serve, who runs the business and how to contact you. This is the page assistants lean on to describe you.
  5. Add structured data. Organization (or LocalBusiness), Service, FAQPage, Article and BreadcrumbList schema tell machines what each page is. Schema does not make weak content rank, but it removes ambiguity.
  6. Make your facts consistent everywhere. Same business name, address, phone, services and opening hours on your site, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Instagram, directories and marketplaces.
  7. Earn independent mentions. Genuine reviews, industry directories, association listings, podcasts, local press and partner pages give assistants the confirmation they look for. You cannot write these yourself, which is exactly why they carry weight.
  8. Keep cornerstone pages fresh. Review your most important pages every quarter, update anything that has changed and show the updated date. Stale prices, discontinued services and old team pages undermine trust.

What does an answer-ready page look like?

Use this checklist on your five most important pages before you write anything new.

  • The first paragraph answers the main question directly and names your business, service and location.
  • Every H2 is a question a buyer would actually type or say.
  • Specifics replace adjectives: sectors, locations, processes, timelines, what is and is not included.
  • Key information is in HTML text, not inside images, sliders, PDFs or chat widgets.
  • There is at least one table, list or step-by-step section that can be lifted cleanly.
  • An FAQ section answers the follow-up questions in self-contained paragraphs.
  • Schema matches what is visible on the page.
  • Internal links connect the page to related services and guides, so engines understand how your expertise fits together.
  • The page shows when it was last updated.

What does AEO look like for a real type of business?

Take a generic example: a dental group with three branches in Dubai. A typical AEO programme for a business like this starts by writing down the forty or so questions patients ask an assistant, from "which clinic near Dubai Marina is good for clear aligners" to "does any clinic in Al Barsha open late on Fridays". Those prompts are run across the main assistants to see who gets named today and which sources are cited.

The fixes usually fall into a few buckets. Each branch gets its own page with its own address, hours and services, matched exactly to its Google Business Profile. Treatment pages are rewritten to lead with the answer patients want: who it suits, how long it takes, what the steps are. An FAQ section covers the questions that come up on WhatsApp every week. The group works on reviews and local listings so independent sources describe each branch consistently. Then the same prompts are rerun every week to see what moved. How do you measure AEO?

You cannot manage what you do not track, and Google Search Console will not tell you whether ChatGPT recommends you. Measurement has to be done on purpose.

  1. Build a prompt set. List the buyer-intent questions your customers ask, grouped by service and location. Include comparison prompts ("X vs Y") and "who is the best" prompts.
  2. Run them on a schedule. Ask the same prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews every week, because answers change.
  3. Record five things. Whether you are mentioned, your position in the list, whether the description is accurate, which competitors appear and which sources the assistant cites.
  4. Turn gaps into tasks. A missing mention becomes a page to write, a wrong fact becomes a listing to correct, a cited directory you are absent from becomes a profile to create.

Doing this by hand is slow, which is why we built our own AI visibility platform. It shows whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews recommend a business for the prompts that matter, and which sources are shaping the answer.

What are the most common AEO mistakes?

  • Blocking AI crawlers without knowing it, through a firewall rule, a CDN setting or a robots.txt line copied from somewhere else.
  • Hiding key facts in images, PDFs, sliders or content that only loads after JavaScript runs.
  • Vague copy such as "we deliver excellence" that gives an assistant nothing to quote.
  • Inconsistent facts, such as an old address on a directory or a service you stopped offering still listed on your profile.
  • No FAQ content, the format answer engines find easiest to reuse.
  • Chasing volume instead of answers. Fifty thin posts do less than five pages that each fully answer one real question.
  • Not measuring. Without a weekly prompt run, you cannot tell whether anything you changed made a difference.

Should you do AEO in-house or bring in help?

Many businesses can handle the basics themselves. Use these criteria to decide.

  • Do it in-house if you have someone who can edit the website, owns your Google Business Profile and directory listings, and has a few hours a week to run prompts and fix what they find.
  • Bring in help if your site has technical problems such as blocked crawlers or JavaScript-only content, if you operate in several locations or languages, if your facts are inconsistent across many listings, or if you need weekly tracking across several assistants.
  • Either way, the people who know the customer's questions are your sales and front-desk teams. Involve them from day one.

If the fixes uncover deeper website problems, such as a site that cannot be crawled or structured properly, that is a web development job rather than a content one, and it is worth solving first.

Frequently asked questions

What is AEO in digital marketing?

AEO, or answer engine optimisation, is the practice of structuring your website and your presence across the web so that AI assistants and search features quote your content as the direct answer and recommend your business by name. It covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Overviews and voice assistants, and it builds on good technical SEO rather than replacing it.

Is AEO the same as GEO?

They overlap almost completely. GEO, generative engine optimisation, usually refers to generative AI tools such as ChatGPT and Perplexity. AEO is a slightly broader term that covers any engine returning a direct answer, including Google AI Overviews and voice assistants. The tactics are the same: clear answers, structure, consistent facts and independent mentions.

How long does AEO take to show results?

It depends on what you change. Fixes to your own site can show up once the engines recrawl those pages, which may be quick for frequently crawled sites and slower for small ones. Changes that depend on third parties, such as reviews, directories and press mentions, take longer to build. That is why weekly tracking matters: it shows which changes moved the answer.

Can AEO hurt my Google rankings?

No. The core AEO practices, such as answering questions directly, using clear headings, adding accurate schema, fixing crawl problems and keeping facts consistent, are also good SEO practice. The only risk is the same as with SEO: publishing large amounts of thin or repetitive content in the hope of being quoted, which tends to weaken a site rather than strengthen it.

Which AI assistants should a business track?

Track the assistants your buyers actually use. For most businesses that means ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, with Copilot added for B2B audiences who work in Microsoft 365. Run the same prompt set across each one, because they retrieve and cite different sources and a business can be recommended by one while missing from another.
