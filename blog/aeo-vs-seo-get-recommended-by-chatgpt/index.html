AEO vs SEO: How to Get Your Business Recommended by ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews

Answer Engine Optimization explained: what AEO is, how it differs from SEO, and the practical steps that make AI assistants cite and recommend your business.

The short answer

SEO helps your pages rank in search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) helps your business become the answer that AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews give. You win at AEO with clear question-and-answer content, structured data (schema), consistent facts about your business across the web, and being mentioned by sources AI models trust.

Why AEO matters now

Buyers increasingly ask an AI assistant before they search: “best dental clinic in JLT”, “who builds WhatsApp bots in Dubai”, “top fire-fighting vehicle manufacturers in India”. The assistant replies with a short list. If you are not on it, you never get the click, because there is no click to get.

SEO vs AEO at a glance

  • Goal: SEO wants a ranking and a click. AEO wants a citation and a recommendation.
  • Unit: SEO optimises pages. AEO optimises answers: individual paragraphs that can be quoted.
  • Signals: SEO leans on links and keywords. AEO leans on clarity, structure, consistency and third-party mentions.

They are not rivals. Strong technical SEO is the foundation AEO is built on.

7 practical AEO steps

  1. Lead with the answer. Put a 2–3 sentence direct answer at the top of every key page and post.
  2. Write in questions. Use the exact questions buyers ask as headings.
  3. Add structured data. Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage and Article schema help machines understand who you are.
  4. Make facts consistent. Same name, address, services and phone on your site, Google Business Profile, directories and social profiles.
  5. Earn mentions. Reviews, industry directories, podcasts and press give AI models independent confirmation.
  6. Publish specifics. Real numbers, locations, industries and processes beat generic marketing copy.
  7. Keep it fresh. Update cornerstone pages every quarter; AI engines favour current information.

How do you measure AEO?

Run the buyer-intent prompts your customers would use across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, every week. Track whether you are mentioned, your position in the list, which competitors appear, and which sources the AI cites. That gives you a visibility score and a clear list of content to fix. We run this kind of AI-visibility audit for clinics, jewellers and service businesses in Dubai and India.

Common AEO mistakes

  • Hiding key information in images, PDFs or JavaScript that crawlers cannot read.
  • Vague “we deliver excellence” copy with no concrete claims.
  • Blocking AI crawlers in robots.txt without meaning to.
  • No FAQ content, the format answer engines quote most easily.

Frequently asked questions

What is AEO in digital marketing?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring content so AI assistants and search features quote it as the direct answer, and recommend your business by name.

Is AEO the same as GEO?

They overlap. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on generative AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity; AEO covers any engine that returns a direct answer, including Google AI Overviews and voice assistants. The tactics are largely the same.

Does SEO still matter if people use ChatGPT?

Yes. Many AI assistants retrieve live web results, so pages that are crawlable, fast and well-structured are the ones they find and cite.
