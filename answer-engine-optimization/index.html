In short

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of making a business easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews to understand, trust and recommend. It covers consistent entity facts, structured data, pages that answer buyer questions directly, credible third-party mentions and access for AI crawlers. What is answer engine optimization?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of making your business the one AI assistants name when a buyer asks a question. When someone asks ChatGPT "who builds WhatsApp agents for clinics in Dubai" or asks Google and gets an AI Overview at the top, there is no page of ten blue links to compete on. There is a short answer that names a few businesses. AEO is about being in that answer, described accurately.

You will also see it called generative engine optimization (GEO) or AI SEO. The labels differ; the work is the same. It sits on top of good search engine optimisation rather than replacing it, and it rewards businesses that are clear, consistent and corroborated across the web.

How do ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews choose who to recommend?

Each assistant works slightly differently, but the pattern is similar. For questions about current businesses and services, the assistant searches the web, reads a handful of sources and writes an answer from them. What it already learned in training shapes which names feel familiar. What it retrieves at the moment of the question decides what it actually says.

  • Google AI Overviews draw on Google's own search index, so normal crawlability and indexing are the entry ticket.
  • ChatGPT search and Perplexity run their own crawlers and also draw on search indexes, which is why being findable beyond Google matters.
  • Gemini can ground its answers in Google Search, so the same foundations that serve AI Overviews apply.

Across all of them, sources get used when they answer the question directly, state facts plainly, agree with what other sources say and can be fetched without obstacles. A beautiful website that says "we deliver excellence" and hides its services behind animations gives an assistant nothing to quote.

What does AEO work involve?

Entity clarity

An assistant needs to know exactly who you are: one name, one description, the services you offer, where you operate and who you serve. That information should be identical on your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, directories and anywhere else you are listed. Inconsistencies, such as an old address or a service you stopped offering, make the assistant less confident and less likely to name you.

Structured data

Schema markup tells machines what a page is about in a format they do not have to guess. The core set for most service businesses: Organization or LocalBusiness with sameAs links to your official profiles, Service for each offering, FAQPage where you answer questions, Article for guides and Person for founders or experts. It does not make weak content rank, but it removes ambiguity from good content.

Quotable answers

Write pages the way assistants read them. Use the buyer's question as the heading, answer it in the first two or three sentences, then give the detail. Each answer should make sense if lifted out on its own. Include the specifics that make an answer useful: locations, sectors, timelines, how the service works. A page with a clear answer of a few dozen words under a question heading is far easier to cite than a long paragraph that gets there eventually.

Third-party mentions

Assistants trust what other sources say about you more than what you say about yourself. Genuine reviews, industry directories, association listings, podcasts, local press and partner pages all count. The aim is not volume but corroboration: several independent sources describing you the same way you describe yourself.

Crawlability for AI bots

Check your robots.txt and firewall rules. AI crawlers such as GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot from OpenAI, PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot are often blocked by default security settings without the business knowing. Google-Extended controls whether your content is used to train and ground Gemini models, but blocking it does not remove you from AI Overviews, which follow normal Google crawling. Many AI crawlers fetch the raw HTML and do not run JavaScript reliably, so content that only appears after scripts load may be invisible to them. Server-rendered pages solve that.

llms.txt

llms.txt is a proposed convention: a plain text file at the root of your site that summarises who you are and points to your most important pages in a form language models can read quickly. Adoption by the major assistants is not confirmed, so treat it as cheap housekeeping, not a ranking lever. Anyone selling it as the secret to AI visibility is overselling.

How do you measure answer engine optimization?

Rankings do not tell you whether an assistant recommends you. You need to ask the assistants directly, in a consistent way, over time:

  1. Build a prompt set. Write the questions real buyers ask, by service, sector and location: "best accounting firm for small businesses in Dubai", "who makes custom diamond jewellery in Mumbai", "how do I automate invoice entry into Tally".
  2. Run them regularly across the assistants that matter to your buyers.
  3. Record the outcome: whether you are named, how you are described, which competitors appear and which sources are cited.
  4. Trace the sources. The cited pages show you where assistants are getting their information, and which directories or articles you need to appear in.
  5. Fix and repeat. Change one thing at a time where you can, and watch the trend rather than any single answer.

Answers vary from one run to the next, so a single screenshot proves very little. A repeated, structured check does. AEO vs SEO: what actually changes?

SEO aims for a ranking and a click. AEO aims for a mention and a recommendation inside an answer. The foundations overlap heavily: a crawlable, fast site with clear pages helps both. What AEO adds is a sharper focus on direct answers, entity consistency and independent corroboration, and a different way of measuring success. We cover the differences in detail in AEO vs SEO: how to get recommended by ChatGPT.

How to rank in ChatGPT: a practical first month

A typical starting plan for a services business, such as a dental and aesthetic clinic group in Dubai or a bookkeeping firm in Sydney:

  1. Week one: baseline. Build the prompt set, run it, and record where you appear and who appears instead.
  2. Week one: unblock. Fix robots.txt, firewall and rendering problems so assistants can read the site.
  3. Week two: entity clean-up. Align your name, services and locations across the website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and key directories. Add Organization and Service markup.
  4. Weeks two and three: answer pages. Rewrite the main service and location pages to answer the buyer's question first, with FAQs that reflect real follow-up questions.
  5. Week four: corroboration. List the sources assistants cite for your prompts and work out how to earn an accurate presence on them.
  6. Then: measure monthly and publish guides that fill the gaps the prompt set reveals.

Our own guides and articles follow the same structure we recommend: a direct answer at the top, question headings and specific detail underneath.

Frequently asked questions

How long does answer engine optimization take to show results?

Fixes such as unblocking AI crawlers or correcting inconsistent business details can change what assistants say within weeks, as they recrawl. Earning third-party mentions and building strong answer pages takes longer and compounds over months. Because assistant answers vary between runs, judge progress on a repeated prompt set over time rather than on any single answer.

Can you guarantee my business will be recommended by ChatGPT?

No, and nobody honestly can. AI assistants decide what to say for each question, and their answers change over time. What we can do is remove the reasons they leave you out, such as blocked crawlers, unclear pages, inconsistent facts and missing corroboration, and then measure whether you are being named more often for the questions your buyers ask.

Is generative engine optimization different from AEO?

In practice they describe the same work. Generative engine optimization, or GEO, emphasises being cited inside AI-generated answers, while answer engine optimization is the broader term for getting your business into answers from assistants and search features. Both rely on the same foundations: clear facts, structured data, quotable pages, credible mentions and crawl access.

Should I block AI crawlers from my website?

For most service businesses, no. If you want assistants to recommend you, they need to read your site. Blocking crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot reduces the chance of being cited. Some businesses choose to block training crawlers while allowing search crawlers. Check your firewall too, because security settings sometimes block these bots without anyone deciding to.

Do I still need SEO if I invest in AEO?

Yes. Google AI Overviews draw on Google's search index, and other assistants also lean on search indexes and crawled pages. A site that is slow, poorly structured or hard to crawl struggles in both. Think of SEO as the foundation and AEO as the layer that makes your content easy for assistants to quote and recommend.

Does answer engine optimization work for local businesses?

Yes, and local service businesses often have the most to gain because buyers ask assistants for recommendations in a specific city or area. Accurate Google Business Profile details, consistent listings, genuine reviews and location pages that answer local questions directly all help assistants connect your business to the places you serve.
