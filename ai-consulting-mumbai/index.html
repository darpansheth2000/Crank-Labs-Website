What does the Indian mid-market actually run on?

Across manufacturers, distributors, hospitals and service firms in India, the same patterns repeat:

  • Finance runs on Tally. Purchase bills, sales invoices and journal entries are keyed in by an accounts team, often from PDFs, photos or paper. GST compliance adds its own layer of checks and reconciliations.
  • Sales runs on WhatsApp. Dealers, distributors and customers message salespeople on personal numbers. Quotes go out as PDFs or photos of a price list. When a salesperson leaves, the customer history often leaves too.
  • Reporting runs on Excel. MIS is assembled by hand from Tally exports and sales sheets, and arrives after the decisions it should have informed.
  • Approvals run through the owner. Many decisions wait for one or two people, usually by phone or WhatsApp.
  • Teams work in several languages. English, Hindi, Marathi and others, often within the same conversation.

Our view is that the answer is almost never to rip out Tally or push everyone onto a new ERP. It is to connect what exists, remove the re-keying and put the right information in front of the right person at the right time.

Two worked examples

A manufacturer with a dealer network

A 150-person manufacturer selling through dealers across western India typically receives orders on WhatsApp, re-types them into a spreadsheet for production, raises invoices in Tally once goods are dispatched and chases payments by phone. The owner gets a sales and outstanding report once a month. The first build is usually a dealer order flow on one official WhatsApp number that captures each order in a structured form and confirms stock and dispatch dates. The second connects dispatch to Tally, so invoices are drafted automatically and approved by accounts. The third is a daily view of orders, dispatches and receivables by dealer.

A hospital with a maternity wing

A multi-specialty hospital with a maternity wing typically fields a steady stream of calls and WhatsApp messages about doctor availability, delivery packages, visiting hours and reports. The front desk answers the same questions all day while new patients wait. The first build is an agent that answers from information the hospital has approved, books OPD appointments, sends reminders and passes anything clinical or urgent to staff immediately. Patient data stays on controlled infrastructure, and nothing clinical goes out without a person approving it.

What does India's DPDP Act 2023 mean for your AI systems?

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 sets out how businesses, called data fiduciaries in the Act, may process personal data. In plain terms it expects clear notice and consent, use limited to the stated purpose, reasonable security safeguards, notification of breaches, and respect for people's rights to access, correct and erase their data. The detailed obligations depend on the rules made under the Act, so check the current position with your legal adviser.

For an AI build, that translates into a short checklist:

  1. Know what personal data each agent or workflow touches, and why.
  2. Collect consent where it is needed, especially for marketing messages on WhatsApp, and honour withdrawals promptly.
  3. Limit access: the order agent sees order data, not salaries or patient records.
  4. Keep data on infrastructure Crank Labs controls or on your own cloud, with every action logged.
  5. For hospitals and finance, put a human approval step before anything is sent or posted.

This is how we engineer systems, not legal advice.

How to choose an AI automation agency in Mumbai

Questions to ask before you sign with anyone offering AI consulting in India:

  • Will it post directly into TallyPrime, or produce a file someone has to import by hand?
  • Do you use the official WhatsApp Business Platform, or a bulk-messaging tool that puts our number at risk?
  • Can the agent handle Hindi and Marathi as well as English?
  • Who owns the code, workflows and data if we stop working together?
  • What will you measure, and what is our baseline today?
  • Who supports it after go-live, and how quickly?

Red flags: a promise to replace your accounts team, a platform licence dressed up as a strategy, no human approval on finance postings, and a demo built on sample data rather than your own invoices and chats.

Can AI post entries directly into Tally?

Yes. A well-built workflow reads purchase bills, invoices or bank statements, matches them to the right ledgers and parties, and posts them into TallyPrime, so nobody imports a file by hand. For anything financial, an accounts person reviews and approves before the entry is posted. Exceptions, such as a new supplier or a mismatched amount, are flagged for a person rather than guessed.
