Questions, answered

What does an AI consulting firm do?

An AI consulting firm works out where AI will genuinely save time or make money in your business, then helps you get it working. At Crank Labs that means an operations audit, a prioritised list of use cases, and then building and running the agents, automations and software ourselves, with offices in Dubai and Mumbai. You get working systems, not a strategy deck.

What is full stack consulting?

Full stack consulting means one team covers the whole business change: strategy and process design, the software and integrations, AI agents and automation, and the rollout with your people. You never hand a consultant’s recommendations to a separate development agency and hope they survive the translation.

What does business transformation involve?

Business transformation is changing how a company operates so it grows without adding the same amount of cost and headcount. In practice we map how work flows today, redesign the slow or manual parts, build the systems that run the new process, and train your team on it. Most engagements start with one function, such as sales follow-up or finance operations, and expand from there.

How is Crank Labs different from a consulting firm or a dev agency?

Consulting firms advise and leave; development agencies build what they are told. We do both, so the people who diagnose your business are the people who build and run the fix, and we are measured on whether it works in your operations, not on the deck or the code alone.

What does an AI automation agency actually do?

An AI automation agency designs, builds and runs AI agents and workflows that take repetitive work off your team: answering leads on WhatsApp, booking appointments, reading invoices, updating your CRM and reporting. Crank Labs does this end to end: audit, build, integration and ongoing management.

What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions from a script. An AI agent understands intent, makes decisions and takes actions in your systems. It can qualify a lead, check availability, book the slot, send the confirmation and log it in your CRM without a human in the loop.

How much does it cost to build an AI agent for my business?

It depends on how many systems the agent connects to and how much volume it handles. Most of our clients start with one focused agent on a fixed build fee plus a monthly managed plan. You get an exact quote after the free audit, with no surprises.

How long does it take to go live?

A focused agent, such as a WhatsApp lead-response agent or an invoice-entry workflow, is typically live in two to four weeks. Larger multi-system builds are delivered in phases so you see results from the first month.

Which industries do you work with?

Healthcare (hospitals, dental and aesthetic clinics), manufacturing (including fire-fighting vehicle builders), accounting and bookkeeping firms, jewellery and diamond trade, smart-home and electrical contractors, telecom, hospitality and travel. If your team handles enquiries, documents or repeat follow-ups, we can automate it.

Can the AI work with WhatsApp, Tally, Zoho and our existing CRM?

Yes. We integrate with the WhatsApp Business Platform, Microsoft 365 and Gmail, TallyPrime, Zoho Books, popular CRMs, calendars and custom databases. We build around the tools your team already uses instead of replacing them.

Is our customer data safe?

Agents run on infrastructure we control or on your own cloud, with access limited to what each workflow needs. For finance and healthcare workflows we keep a human approval step before anything is sent or posted.

What is AEO and why does my business need it?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring your website and content so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews quote and recommend you. More buyers now ask an AI before they search. If you are not in the answer, you are not on the shortlist.
